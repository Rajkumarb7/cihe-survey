Research Objective

Why This Survey Matters

This survey is part of a quantitative research study investigating the relationship between self-reported weekly study hours and two key outcomes — academic satisfaction and perceived stress — among undergraduate students at CIHE Australia.

Findings will be used to develop evidence-based recommendations for institutional policy and student support programmes. Results are presented in aggregate and anonymously as part of the RES 901 capstone assessment.

Your honest responses matter. There are no right or wrong answers — only your real experience counts. The survey takes around 5 minutes.

📋

Informed Consent

By completing this survey you confirm that: you are a current CIHE Australia student; participation is voluntary and you may stop at any time; your responses are fully anonymous; and data will be used solely for academic research purposes.
