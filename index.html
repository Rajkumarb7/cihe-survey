Research Objective

Why This Study Matters

This survey is part of a quantitative research study investigating the relationship between self-reported weekly study hours and two key student outcomes — academic satisfaction and perceived stress — among undergraduate students at CIHE Australia.

The findings will help identify patterns in student engagement and wellbeing, with the goal of informing evidence-based institutional policies and support programmes. Results will be presented anonymously in an academic research report as part of the RES 901 unit assessment.

Your honest responses are invaluable. The survey takes approximately 5 minutes to complete. All data is collected anonymously — your participant ID is auto-generated and cannot be traced back to you.

📋

Informed Consent

By completing and submitting this survey, you confirm that: (1) you are a current CIHE Australia student; (2) you understand your participation is entirely voluntary and you may stop at any time; (3) your responses are anonymous and no personally identifiable information is collected; and (4) your data will be used solely for academic research purposes within this unit. This study adheres to the CIHE Australia Research Ethics Guidelines and the Australian National Statement on Ethical Conduct in Human Research (NHMRC, 2023).
